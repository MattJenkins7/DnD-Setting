Inside the main burial chamber, there are two verdant, moss encased chests, the contents are as follows:
## **Wand of Conducting**

**Rarity:** Common  
**Type:** Wand  
**Value:** 50 gp  
**Attunement:** None  
**Text:**  
This wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 charge to create orchestral music by waving it around. The music can be heard out to 60 feet and ends when you stop waving the wand.  
The wand regains all charges at dawn.  
If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, a sad tuba sound plays as the wand crumbles to dust.

**Type:** Wondrous Item  
**Value:** 58 gp  
**Attunement:** None  
**Text:**

## **Guardian Emblem**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item  
**Value:** 370 gp  
**Attunement:** Requires a cleric or paladin  
**Text:**  
This emblem is the symbol of a deity or spiritual tradition. As an action, you can attach it to a suit of armor or a shield (or remove it).  
It has 3 charges. When you or a creature you can see within 30 ft suffers a critical hit while wearing or wielding the item bearing the emblem, you can use your reaction to expend 1 charge and turn the critical hit into a normal hit.  
Regains all charges at dawn.

## **Astral Shard**

**Rarity:** Rare  
**Type:** Wondrous Item  
**Value:** 1828 gp  
**Attunement:** Requires a sorcerer  
**Text:**  
A solidified shard of the Astral Plane swirling with silver mist.  
As an action, attach the shard to a Tiny object (such as a weapon or piece of jewelry) or detach it. It falls off if attunement ends.  
You can use the shard as a spellcasting focus.  
When you use a Metamagic option on a spell, immediately after casting the spell you can teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 30 ft.

---

## **Gateway Ring**

**Rarity:** Very Rare  
**Type:** Ring  
**Value:** 1673 gp  
**Attunement:** None  
**Text:**  
Made from tiny moss-covered stones and infused with ancient fey magic.  
As an action, speak the ring’s command word and name a precise location on any plane of existence, then toss it within 10 ft.  
It expands into a 5-foot circular floating stone portal for up to 1 minute, duplicating the _gate_ spell (but cannot target a creature, only a precise location on the same plane).  
You may end the effect early by touching the ring and speaking its command word.  
The ring cannot be used again for 10 days after activation.
