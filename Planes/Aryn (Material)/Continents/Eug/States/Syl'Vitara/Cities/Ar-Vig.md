Small town in the forest with population around 1400 north by about 8km from Keepers sanctuary

#### Attractions:
**Tavern** - The Silent Satyr, bartender a man named Molla Yuret
**Tailor** - Galear's Garments, ran by Galear Rothe
**Barracks** - Captain Elan Thirwell
**Town hall** - Library and area of local government
**Baker** - Brilleth's baked goods, ran by Brilleth Spon
**Market Square** - Local stalls that change
**Blacksmith** - Nameless
**Alchemist** - Fizzle and Snap
