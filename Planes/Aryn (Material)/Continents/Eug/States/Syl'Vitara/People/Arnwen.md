SPECIAL TRAITS

- **Ageless and Ancient**. A chiron never suffers ill effects from aging and cannot die of old age. Magical aging effects have no effect against them, and they gain advantage on saving throws against spells and effects that cause death.
- **Archery**. A chiron gains a +2 bonus with attacks it makes with ranged weapons.
- **Bow of Victory**. A chiron’s quiver never runs out of arrows during combat, producing as many as needed. A chiron’s bow attacks ignore [damage resistance](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Damage-Resistance-and-Vulnerability) and count as magical. As an action, a chiron create a longbow (this causes a previously created bow to vanish). A chiron’s bow attacks deal an additional 1d8 damage (included in the stats below).
- **Magic Resistance**. The chiron has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

ACTIONS

- **Multiattack**. The chiron makes three attacks with its longbow.
- **Hooves**. _Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit_: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- **Longbow**. _Ranged Weapon Attack_: +10 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. _Hit_: 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.
- **Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)**. For 1 minute, the chiron can utter a special command or warning whenever a nonhostile creature that it can see within 30 feet of it makes an [attack](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Attack) roll or a saving throw. The creature can add a d4 to its roll provided it can hear and understand the chiron. A creature can benefit from only one Leadership die at a time. This effect ends if the chiron is [incapacitated](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/conditions/#Incapacitated).
- **Innate Spellcasting**. The chiron’s innate spellcasting ability is [Charisma](https://www.5esrd.com/using-ability-scores#TOC-Charisma) (spell save DC 15). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
    - **At will**: [guidance](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/g/guidance), [minor illusion](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/m/minor-illusion), [true strike](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/t/true-strike)
    - **3/day each**: [augury](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/a/augury), [cure wounds](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/c/cure-wounds), [speak with animals](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/s/speak-with-animals), [tongues](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/t/tongues)
    - **1/day each**: [bless](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/a/bless), [divination](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/d/divination), [heroism](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/h/heroism), [legend lore](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/l/legend-lore), [speak with plants](https://www.5esrd.com/spellcasting/all-spells/s/speak-with-plants)
